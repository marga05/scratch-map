                  Backup

                  1. Tap Prepare backup. It packs everything into one file.
                  2. Tap Save / share → choose Save to Files → iCloud Drive (or AirDrop to your laptop).

                  Also keep the raw Scoria .sqlite in iCloud Drive or on your laptop — it's your master copy.

                  Monthly reminder

                  Adds a repeating event (1st of every month) to your calendar with the export-and-backup checklist.

                  Restore
